# 📅 Day 1 — Cybersecurity Fundamentals

## 🔐 What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting **computers, networks, applications, systems, and data** from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or disruption.

### Main Goals of Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting:

* **Confidentiality** — Prevent unauthorized access to information
* **Integrity** — Prevent unauthorized modification of information
* **Availability** — Ensure systems and data are available when needed

These three principles are known as the **CIA Triad**.

---

## 🔒 What is Information Security?

Information Security (InfoSec) is the practice of protecting information and information systems from:

* Unauthorized access
* Unauthorized modification
* Destruction
* Disclosure
* Disruption

### Cybersecurity vs Information Security

**Cybersecurity** mainly focuses on protecting digital systems, networks, devices, and digital data.

**Information Security** has a broader focus on protecting information, whether it is stored digitally, physically, or transmitted.

# ⚔️ Types of Cyber Attacks

## 1. Malware

Malware means **Malicious Software** designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to systems.

Examples:

* Virus
* Worm
* Trojan
* Ransomware
* Spyware
* Rootkit

---

## 2. Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering attack where an attacker attempts to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as:

* Usernames
* Passwords
* Bank details
* OTPs
* Other confidential information

Example:

An attacker sends a fake banking email containing a malicious link that looks like a legitimate banking website.

---

## 3. Denial-of-Service (DoS)

A DoS attack attempts to make a system, server, or service unavailable to legitimate users by overwhelming or exhausting its resources.

---

## 4. Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS)

A DDoS attack is similar to DoS, but the attack traffic comes from **multiple compromised systems**.

```text
Multiple compromised systems
          ↓
       Server
          ↓
     Service becomes
       unavailable
```

---

## 5. Man-in-the-Middle (MITM)

An attacker secretly intercepts communication between two parties.

```text
User  ←→  Attacker  ←→  Server
```

The attacker may attempt to observe or manipulate the communication.

---

## 6. Password Attacks

Attackers may attempt to obtain or guess passwords using techniques such as:

* Brute Force
* Dictionary Attack
* Credential Stuffing
* Password Spraying

---

## 7. Social Engineering

Social engineering attacks manipulate people rather than directly exploiting technical vulnerabilities.

Examples:

* Phishing
* Vishing
* Smishing
* Pretexting
* Baiting

---

## 8. SQL Injection

SQL Injection is a web application attack where malicious SQL input is supplied to an application in an attempt to manipulate database queries.

It can potentially lead to:

* Unauthorized data access
* Data modification
* Data deletion

---

## 9. Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)

XSS is a web security vulnerability where malicious scripts can be injected into web pages and executed in a victim's browser.

---

## 10. Insider Threat

An insider threat occurs when someone with legitimate access to an organization's systems or data misuses that access.

---

# 🧠 Key Takeaways

* Cybersecurity protects digital systems and information.
* Information Security focuses on protecting information and information systems.
* The **CIA Triad** represents Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
* Cyber attacks can target people, applications, networks, devices, and data.
* Understanding attack types is an important foundation for learning cybersecurity.
